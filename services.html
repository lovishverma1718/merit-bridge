Questions

Good to know.

Can't find what you're looking for? Our team is happy to help.

Ask us anything
How quickly can we get started?
Most programs launch within two to three weeks of our first consultation. Simple overflow or after-hours coverage can often go live faster. We'll give you a clear timeline in your proposal.
Will your agents sound like part of our company?
Yes. Agents answer in your business name, follow scripts and tone guidelines you approve, and are trained on your products, policies and FAQs before they speak with a single customer.
Do you work with our existing software?
In most cases, yes. We work inside the CRM, help desk, booking and phone systems you already use, so your data stays in one place and you keep full visibility.
How is pricing structured?
Pricing depends on the services, coverage hours and team model you choose. After a free consultation we'll send a transparent, custom quote — no hidden fees.
How do we know it's working?
We agree on service-level targets up front and share regular reports on volumes, response times, resolutions and quality scores. Your account lead reviews results with you and recommends improvements.
Can you cover evenings, weekends and holidays?
Yes. Coverage is flexible — from business hours to after-hours, weekends, holidays or full 24/7 support — and can change as your needs do.
